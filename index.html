    What the research told us

    Built from our survey synthesis, journey map and persona (The New Explorer, a first-year B.Tech student).

    Wayfinding confusion

    Blocks look alike and the layout is large, so new students ask many people for directions.

    Signage gaps

    Signs are unclear or inconsistently placed, and there is no central map at key points.

    Digital need

    Students want search, real-time directions, landmarks and an estimated time.

    Real impact

    The confusion causes frustration and lost time, and sometimes late arrival.

    INT335 Design Thinking, Group 13. Ketan Rana, Aditya Roy, Abhi Kapoor, Sreenanda.
    Chosen concept: searchable campus map with real-time wayfinding, walking time and landmarks.
